HistAct
=======

HistAct records the process of historical research (who used which sources, what work was done,
and what came out of it) as linked data, on top of GakuNin RDM projects.

Identifiers of records:
* `https://w3id.org/histact/data/<project>/<kind>/<uuid>` (for example `.../activities/<uuid>`)
* `https://w3id.org/histact/data/<project>/` (the project's named graph)

The rest of https://w3id.org/histact/ is reserved for the HistAct vocabulary.
