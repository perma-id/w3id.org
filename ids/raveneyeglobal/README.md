# /raveneyeglobal

Persistent identifiers for Raveneye Global (the trading name of RavenGroup Global Tech Private Limited) and its published research studies. The identifiers are embedded in the schema.org JSON-LD of https://raveneyeglobal.com/ and redirect to pages on that site.

## Identifiers

* https://w3id.org/raveneyeglobal/ redirects to https://raveneyeglobal.com/
* https://w3id.org/raveneyeglobal/org redirects to https://raveneyeglobal.com/about/
* https://w3id.org/raveneyeglobal/research/ redirects to https://raveneyeglobal.com/research/
* https://w3id.org/raveneyeglobal/research/{slug} redirects to https://raveneyeglobal.com/research/{slug}/
