# /salish-sea/

Permanent identifiers for the Salish Sea Animals register, a published register of individual killer whales and the social groups they belong to in the Salish Sea.

`https://w3id.org/salish-sea/animals/<seven digits>` identifies one animal or group in the register. Every address under `https://w3id.org/salish-sea/` redirects to <https://github.com/salish-sea/animals#identifiers>.

Documentation: <https://github.com/salish-sea/animals>
