# /salish-sea/

Permanent identifiers for the Salish Sea Animals register, a published register of individual killer whales and the social groups they belong to in the Salish Sea, and for the occurrence dataset SalishSea.io publishes.

- `https://w3id.org/salish-sea/animals/<seven digits>` identifies one animal or group in the register, and redirects to <https://github.com/salish-sea/animals#identifiers>.
- `https://w3id.org/salish-sea/datasets/occurrences-v1` identifies SalishSea.io's dataset of marine mammal occurrences, and redirects to its page, <https://salishsea.io/datasets/occurrences-v1>.

Every other address under `https://w3id.org/salish-sea/` redirects to <https://github.com/salish-sea/animals#identifiers>.

Documentation: <https://github.com/salish-sea/animals> and <https://salishsea.io/datasets/occurrences-v1>
