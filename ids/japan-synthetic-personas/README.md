# /japan-synthetic-personas/

Japan Synthetic Consumer Personas (N=3,000), Linked Data edition: 3,000
statistically grounded synthetic Japanese consumer personas, each with a
permanent URI, linked to the e-Stat Statistics LOD (standard area codes for
prefectures, Japan Standard Industrial Classification for industries).
Published as open data under CC BY 4.0.

* Site: https://furuchanchan.github.io/japan-synthetic-personas/lod/
* Source: https://github.com/furuchanchan/japan-synthetic-personas
* Data on Hugging Face: https://huggingface.co/datasets/furuchanchan/japan-synthetic-personas
* Vocabulary: `https://w3id.org/japan-synthetic-personas/ns#`
* Personas: `https://w3id.org/japan-synthetic-personas/id/persona/<uuid>`

Content negotiation: `text/turtle` and `application/ld+json` are served for
personas and the dataset; `text/turtle` for the vocabulary.
