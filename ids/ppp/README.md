# /ppp/

Permanent identifiers for PPP (Propriétés Perceptuelles Pertinentes / Relevant Perceptual Properties), a SKOS vocabulary of perceptual qualities, published under CC BY 4.0.

`https://w3id.org/ppp/` and the identifiers under it redirect (303) to the published site for browsers (`text/html` and any other request), or to the SKOS export by content negotiation: `text/turtle` to `ppp.ttl`, `application/ld+json` to `ppp.jsonld`. The export files requested by name (`https://w3id.org/ppp/ppp.ttl`, `https://w3id.org/ppp/ppp.jsonld`) are redirected with 302.

- Site: <https://blomig.github.io/PPP-ontologie/>
- Export: <https://blomig.github.io/PPP-ontologie/export/ppp.ttl>, <https://blomig.github.io/PPP-ontologie/export/ppp.jsonld>
