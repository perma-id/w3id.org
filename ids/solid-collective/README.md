# solid-collective

The vocabulary of Solid collectives: a group described by a public
`config.ttl` on its own pod, its roster, the folders members share with its
agent. Namespace `https://w3id.org/solid-collective/vocab#`.

- Source: https://github.com/nicolasdb/solid-kit/tree/main/docs/vocab
- Protocol: solid-kit ADR 006
