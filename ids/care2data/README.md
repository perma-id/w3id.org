# /care2data

Persistent identifiers for the ontologies and vocabularies published by [Care2Data](https://care2data.com).

`https://w3id.org/care2data/<path>` redirects to `https://care2data.github.io/ns/<path>`.

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `ctx/` | Terms of Care2Data's contextualisation registry: ISO/IEC 11179 metadata elements that extend the SOLICIT ontologies of the European Commission's Joint Research Centre. These identifiers are Care2Data's, not the JRC's. |

Content negotiation (Turtle, RDF/XML, HTML) will be added when the first releases are published.
