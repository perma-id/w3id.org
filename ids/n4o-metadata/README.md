Redirections for metadata artefacts of NFDI4Objects.

`prov-matrix/` resolves to the Linked-Data view of the catalogue "Matrix: Tools
und Services für Sammlungs- und Provenienzforschung", published by the
Temporary Working Group "Services and Tools for Research Collections and
Provenance Research".

Content negotiation is supported: `text/turtle` and `application/ld+json` are
served from the same generated view as the HTML pages.

* Repository: https://github.com/n4o-rse/ta2-prov-tools-services-matrix
* Archived: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17151594
