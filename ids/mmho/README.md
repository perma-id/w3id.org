## Ontology
**Mining and Metallurgy Heritage Ontology (MMHO, 矿冶遗存本体)** — Version 1.6.0

A domain ontology for knowledge organization of pre-Qin and Han dynasty mining and metallurgy heritage sites in China, covering the complete technological chain from ore extraction through smelting and casting to product disposal.
