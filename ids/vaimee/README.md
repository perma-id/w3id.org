# /vaimee/

Permanent identifiers for vocabularies published by [VAIMEE S.r.l.](https://vaimee.com).

`https://w3id.org/vaimee/awm` is the namespace of **AgoràWasteMap**, a vocabulary
for describing organic by-products — what a material is, where it is, how much of
it there is, and which body of law it falls under. It is developed in
AgoràWasteMap, a sub-project of I3-4-BIOFERTILIZERS, co-funded by the European
Union under the Interregional Innovation Investments instrument
(Grant Agreement No 101161143).

| Identifier | Redirects to |
| --- | --- |
| `https://w3id.org/vaimee/awm` | <https://wastemap.vaimee.com/ontology/awm> |
| `https://w3id.org/vaimee/awm/sources` | <https://wastemap.vaimee.com/ontology/awm-sources> |
| `https://w3id.org/vaimee/awm/legal` | <https://wastemap.vaimee.com/ontology/awm-legal> |
| `https://w3id.org/vaimee/awm/shapes` | <https://wastemap.vaimee.com/ontology/awm-shapes> |

Each target is content-negotiated: a browser is served documentation, a client
asking for `text/turtle` is served the Turtle.

Terms are minted in a single namespace, `https://w3id.org/vaimee/awm#`, while the
definitions are written in three modules. The document served at the namespace
IRI is the union of all three, so every term resolves to a document that defines
it. Each module remains retrievable on its own at its own IRI.

Documentation: <https://wastemap.vaimee.com/ontology/>
