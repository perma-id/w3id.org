# /gyomu/

業務語彙（Gyomu Vocabulary）: a vocabulary and reference dataset describing
business operations of companies in Japan in four layers (department, workflow,
stage and step), with the roles, artifacts, judgments and Japanese regulations
attached to each step. Published as open data under CC BY 4.0.

* Site: https://furuchanchan.github.io/gyomu-vocab/
* Source: https://github.com/furuchanchan/gyomu-vocab
* Vocabulary: `https://w3id.org/gyomu/ns#`
* Data: `https://w3id.org/gyomu/id/...`

Content negotiation: `text/turtle` and `application/ld+json` are served for
the data; `text/turtle` for the vocabulary.
