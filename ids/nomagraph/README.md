# /nomagraph/

Permanent identifiers for the Nomagraph legal knowledge graph: statutes,
regulator rulebooks and guidance, case law, defined terms, obligations,
editorial commentary and related entities, with a focus on financial services
law.

Identifiers under this namespace are minted only for entities that have no
official public identifier of their own. Where an official identifier exists
(ELI, legislation.gov.uk, CELEX), it is used verbatim and never re-minted here.
Identifiers are never reused or deleted; retired entities keep their identifier
and resolve to a page saying so.

`https://w3id.org/nomagraph/` redirects to <https://nomagraph.com/id/>.
