# /hfi — Hellenic Folk Instruments (HFI)

Bilingual (el/en) SKOS thesaurus of Greek folk musical instruments, hierarchised
according to the MIMO revised Hornbostel–Sachs classification (2011), with
mappings to the MIMO thesaurus of instrument names.

* Namespace: `https://w3id.org/hfi/`
* Redirects to: `https://data.tropos.org.gr/hfi/` (content negotiation is
  performed at the target: Turtle, RDF/XML, JSON-LD, HTML)
* Maintainer: Dr Ioannis Valiantzas, Laboratory of Ethnomusicology and Cultural
  Anthropology, Department of Music Studies, National and Kapodistrian
  University of Athens — ival@uoa.gr
