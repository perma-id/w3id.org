## About

BIMxel is developed within the **GreenBIMxels** project, led by the
Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC). Source repository (UPC-internal
GitLab): https://mygitlab.cs.upc.edu/GreenBIMxels/ontologia-BIMxels

### Abstract

The BIMxel ontology acts as a semantic bridge between widely adopted
ontologies and standards—including BOT, SAREF, SAREF4ENER, PROV-O, DCAT,
QUDT, GeoSPARQL, and BEO—to support the interoperable generation of building
digital twins. BIMxel integrates BIM data, sensors, measurements, external
data services, and provenance information into a unified knowledge graph,
enabling continuous monitoring of building operation and energy
consumption. By linking heterogeneous sources such as IFC models, IoT
devices and physical properties, the ontology provides a scalable semantic
foundation for assessing operational carbon impact and supporting
data-driven strategies for more sustainable buildings.

### Introduction

The BIMxel ontology was developed within the GreenBIMxels project, an
initiative led by the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC) to
establish a BIM- and AI-based digital information standard for public
buildings and infrastructures. The project aims to create an interoperable
data ecosystem supporting collaboration among public administrations,
academia, technology providers, and facility managers, with a particular
focus on energy efficiency, sustainable water management, and carbon
footprint reduction throughout the building lifecycle.

To support these objectives, BIMxel acts as a semantic bridge between
established ontologies, including BOT, SAREF, SAREF4ENER, PROV-O, DCAT,
QUDT, GeoSPARQL, and BEO. Rather than replacing these standards, it
integrates them into a unified knowledge graph that connects BIM models,
building topology, sensors, measurements, external data services, and
provenance information. This semantic layer enables the automated
generation of interoperable Digital Twins for monitoring building
operation, assessing energy performance, and supporting the reduction of
operational carbon emissions.
